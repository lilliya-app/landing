lilliya

privacy

Effective November 20, 2026  ·  support@lilliya.app

the short version

The Lilliya app does not collect your data. Everything you record and write stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the app makes no internet connections of its own. The only things we ever hold are your email address, if you join the waitlist on this website, and any email you send us.

who we are

Lilliya is made by Marushka Hirshon. You can reach us at support@lilliya.app.

what lilliya keeps, and where

Lilliya keeps the following on your iPhone only:

  • Your recordings: the songs, stories and messages you record, as audio files.
  • What you write: titles, the notes you add to recordings, diary entries, and playlist names and descriptions.
  • What you tell the app about your family: your name, your partner's name, what you call your baby or babies, how many you are expecting, and your due date. Lilliya uses the due date only to work out the pregnancy week shown in the app. If you tell the app that your baby has arrived, or that your pregnancy has ended, it keeps that and the date, so it can stop counting the weeks.
  • Your choices: your playlists, the flower you gave each recording, the light you chose for the app (dawn, day, dusk, night, or following the hour), and whether the weekly reminder is on and when.
  • Suggestions you write in "Make a suggestion", kept on your iPhone until you choose to send them by email.

We have no servers that receive any of this. We cannot see it, hear it, or recover it for you.

what the app collects

Nothing. Lilliya has:

  • no account or sign-in;
  • no analytics or usage tracking;
  • no advertising, and no advertising identifiers;
  • no third-party services that receive your data.

the microphone

Lilliya asks to use the microphone so you can record. It records only while a recording you started is in progress, and the audio is saved on your iPhone.

when information leaves your iphone

Only when you choose to send it:

  • Email. "Make a suggestion" and "Support" open your own Mail app with a draft. If you send it, we receive your email address, what you wrote, the app's version number, and, for a suggestion, your first name if you gave one. We use this only to read and answer your message.
  • Saving a copy. Saving a copy of one recording, or "Save all my recordings", opens Apple's share sheet. The copy goes wherever you send it (Files, iCloud Drive, a message), under that service's own terms. Lilliya does not see where it goes.
  • Links. The privacy page and the terms open in your web browser, on lilliya.app.
  • The weekly reminder. If you turn it on, your iPhone itself shows it at the time you chose. It is not sent to you from anywhere, and Lilliya learns nothing from it.

the waitlist and this website

If you join the waitlist on lilliya.app, we collect your email address and store it with our form provider. We use it only to write to you about Lilliya. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete your address at any time by writing to support@lilliya.app. Our website does not have advertiser trackers.

We keep waitlist emails until you unsubscribe, until you ask us to delete them, or until the waitlist is finished upon launch.

backups

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or to a computer, Apple may include Lilliya's recordings and notes in that backup, as it does for other apps. Those backups belong to your Apple account and are covered by Apple's privacy policy. We have no access to them.

pregnancy and family information

Your due date and anything you record or write about your pregnancy can be sensitive. It stays on your iPhone and is never sent to us or to anyone else by the app.

children

Lilliya is made for parents and other adults. It is not directed at children, and it collects no information from anyone, of any age.

deleting your information

  • Delete any recording, note or playlist inside the app.
  • Account → Delete everything removes all recordings and everything else Lilliya has stored.
  • Deleting the app from your iPhone removes everything stored in it.

Because we hold nothing you made in the app, there is nothing of that for us to delete. If you joined the waitlist or have sent us an email, write to support@lilliya.app and we will delete it.

your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights over personal data that a company holds about you. The app holds none. If you joined the waitlist or have emailed us, you can ask to see, correct or delete that address or correspondence at any time, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

changes to this policy

If Lilliya ever changes how it handles information (for example, by adding optional iCloud syncing), we will update this page and its date before that change reaches you.
